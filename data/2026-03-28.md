Motivation: To develop a cosmological simulation method that accurately models interactions between dark matter and baryons in the challenging regime where dark matter particle mass is comparable to or lighter than baryons.

Method: A hybrid method combining mean-field calculations for gas particle momentum and energy transfer with Monte Carlo scatterings for dark matter particles, derived from the Boltzmann equation and proven statistically equivalent, implemented in the GIZMO code.

Abstract: We report on a new method for incorporating interactions between dark matter (DM) and baryons in cosmological simulations, capable of handling the challenging regime in which the dark matter particle mass is comparable to or lighter than the baryon mass. We present an open-source implementation of this method in the simulation code GIZMO. As a first application, we investigate the effects of DM--baryon interactions on an isolated Milky Way-like disk galaxy for dark matter having twice the proton mass, which roughly maximizes the average energy transfer per collision.
