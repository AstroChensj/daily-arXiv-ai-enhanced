Our model successfully reproduces the observed multiwavelength spectral energy distribution from radio to $γ$ rays and naturally accounts for the source's low radio flux without invoking extra emission zones. We also reproduce the mid-IR emission within the same framework. These results demonstrate that extended jet leptonic models can robustly describe the broadband physics of radio-weak BL Lacs.
